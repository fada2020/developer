Privacy Policy

Hyukju Studio respects user privacy. We collect only the information necessary to provide, maintain, and improve our applications and services. This may include contact information provided by users, technical logs, device information, and usage data required for service operation.

We do not sell personal information. Data may be processed by trusted service providers for hosting, analytics, crash reporting, customer support, or payment processing, only as needed to operate the service. We apply reasonable security measures to protect information from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction.

Users may request access, correction, or deletion of their personal information by contacting us by email. We retain information only for as long as necessary for service operation, legal compliance, dispute resolution, and legitimate business purposes.

This policy may be updated when our services or legal requirements change. The latest version will be posted on this page.
